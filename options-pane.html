About Streamonio

A browser-extension to call HTTP endpoints with page's media URLs (podcasts, radios & live streams).

Supported: HLS (.m3u8), DASH (.mpd), HTTP audio, RTMP/RTSP, Icecast/Shoutcast.

Home: https://github.com/ankostis/streamonio
